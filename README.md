### Why I Started Upper Layer

Most teams lose hours every week to work that shouldn't be done by hand — chasing repetitive tasks, missing incoming calls, and gluing tools together with fragile manual steps.

When companies try to fix this, they usually run into two bad options:
1. **Bloated agencies** that spend months on slide decks and discovery phases before shipping a single line of code.
2. **Subscription traps** where you pay forever per-task inside third-party tools you never truly own.

I founded **Upper Layer Studio** to offer a direct, practical alternative:

- **Direct communication:** A solo studio by design. You talk directly to the person scoping and writing the code. No account managers or lost requirements.
- **Keep what works:** We fix what is slowing you down without forcing you to replace tools your team already uses.
- **Ship fast & small:** A working prototype deployed in weeks rather than months of theory.
- **Total ownership:** You own the code, the prompts, and the infrastructure from day one in your own accounts.

### What I Build

```
01. AI Automation  ──►  02. Voice AI  ──►  03. AI Agents  ──►  04. Product Build
```

#### 01. AI Automation
Workflows that run reliably in the background — with human approval right where it matters.
- Process and workflow audits
- Deep integrations across your existing tools & databases
- Real-time logging, fail-safes, and documentation

#### 02. Voice AI
Custom inbound and outbound voice agents that sound natural, qualify callers, and book appointments directly on your calendar.
- 24/7 call answering on your own number
- Instant CRM logging and post-call summaries
- Seamless hand-off to a human when needed

#### 03. AI Agents
Specialized agents connected to your knowledge base, internal systems, and APIs.
- Retrieval over your company data
- Structured tool use and external actions
- Strict evaluation guardrails to prevent hallucinations

#### 04. Product Build
Full-stack production software for AI-first products and automations that have outgrown third-party tools.
- Modern interface & product design
- Scalable backend, model orchestration & fallbacks
- Full deployment into your cloud environment
